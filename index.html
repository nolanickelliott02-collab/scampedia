How Scampedia Works

From new scam to published article

Every entry starts as a real pattern, gets written up in plain language, and stays connected to live protection.

AI Researches Daily

Once a day, an AI searches real news, FTC, FBI, and BBB sources for a genuinely new scam trend. It never invents one, and skips the day entirely if it doesn't find something solid.

02
✅

Automatically Checked

Every citation is verified before anything gets further: the link has to actually resolve, and the page it points to has to genuinely be about this specific scam, not just exist.

03
🔀

Requires a Merge to Publish

Every entry becomes a pull request, not a live page, until someone reviews and merges it. There's no automatic path around that.
