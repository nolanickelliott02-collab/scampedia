This week's new scams

11 new scams added to Scampedia this week. Tap one to see how it works and the red flags to watch for. Also see every documented scam.

Government Scam Nov 16, 2026

Paid Social Security Card Replacement Website Scam

Scammers run lookalike websites that charge people a fee to apply for, replace, or correct a Social Security card — a service the Social Security Administration always provides for free.
Phone Scam Nov 13, 2026

Navy Federal/Military Bank "Parking Lot" Phone Scam

Scammers approach people in parking lots near military banks like Navy Federal Credit Union or USAA, claim to be in an emergency, and ask to "borrow" the victim's phone — then secretly use the victim's own banking app to drain accounts or take out loans in their name.
Phone Scam Nov 12, 2026

Hijacked Mass-Texting Platform / Trusted Short Code Scam

Hackers breach a third-party mass-texting company and use its trusted short codes to blast scam texts that appear to come from legitimate senders like state government alert systems, charities, or advocacy groups.
Government Scam Nov 12, 2026

F-1 Visa Status Impersonation Scam Targeting Middle Eastern Students

Scammers impersonate U.S. or foreign immigration/government officials to tell foreign students on F-1 visas they are "out of status" and must pay or share personal information immediately. The FBI says the scheme has specifically targeted students from the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Jordan.
Shopping Scam Nov 12, 2026

Fake FIFA World Cup Website Spoofing Scam

The FBI warns that cyber criminals are spoofing FIFA's official website with lookalike domains to steal personal data and sell fake 2026 World Cup tickets and hospitality packages.
Government Scam Nov 11, 2026

AI-Cloned Fake Immigration Attorney Scam

Scammers use AI to clone the face, voice, and even signature of real, licensed immigration attorneys and law firms, then run fake social media ads and websites promising guaranteed green cards, work permits, or citizenship to lure fearful immigrants into paying thousands for services that never materialize.
Shopping Scam Nov 17, 2026

Addiction Treatment Center Impersonation Lead-Gen Scam

Dishonest marketers buy Google search ads using the names of real, well-known substance use disorder treatment clinics, then route callers to their own unaffiliated call centers that pretend to be the clinic the person was searching for.
Phone Scam Nov 15, 2026

Phone Return Scam (Fake Mobile Upgrade / Redirected Device)

Scammers call pretending to be your mobile carrier offering a free phone upgrade, then after a real device is delivered to your home, trick you into "returning" it using a fake shipping label that sends it straight to the criminals — leaving you stuck with the bill.
AI Scam Nov 14, 2026

"FaceStealer" North Korean Fake Video Call Crypto Theft Scam

North Korean hackers hijack a victim's messaging accounts, then use stolen webcam recordings (and sometimes AI deepfakes) of that victim to lure their contacts into fake video-conferencing calls that trick them into installing crypto-draining malware.
Shopping Scam Nov 14, 2026

Wedding Photographer Deposit Scam

Couples book a wedding photographer they found on social media or a wedding vendor site, pay a deposit through a peer-to-peer app, and then the photographer cancels at the last minute, never shows up, or never delivers the photos.
Shopping Scam Nov 10, 2026

Counterfeit Ozempic / Semaglutide Weight-Loss Drug Scam

Scammers and rogue online sellers exploit the surging demand for Ozempic, Wegovy, and other semaglutide weight-loss drugs by peddling counterfeit or unapproved versions through fake online pharmacies, social media ads, med spas, and "weight loss clinics" — often with no prescription required.
