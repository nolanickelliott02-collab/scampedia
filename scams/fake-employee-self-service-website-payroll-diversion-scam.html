Fake Employee Self-Service Website Payroll Diversion Scam

Added Nov 22, 2026· 🤖 AI-researched· 🏛️ Government-Verified· Editor: Nick Elliott· Source: FBI/IC3 Public Service Announcement

Overview

The FBI warns that criminals are buying fraudulent search engine ads that mimic employee self-service websites (payroll, unemployment, health savings, and retirement account portals) to steal login credentials and redirect victims' direct deposits to accounts the scammers control.

How It Works

According to an FBI/IC3 Public Service Announcement, the FBI is warning the public that cyber criminals are targeting users of employee self-service websites owned by companies and government services, using search engine advertisements to impersonate legitimate websites and steal victim information and funds.

Red Flags

  • A website reached through a sponsored/ad search result rather than a bookmark or typed address
  • Login page URL has a minor misspelling or extra character compared to the real employee portal address
  • Unexpected prompts to re-enter banking, direct deposit, or login credentials on a payroll, unemployment, HSA, or retirement account site
  • Sudden, unauthorized changes to direct deposit or banking information on a payroll or benefits account

Real Examples

  • FBI/IC3 PSA (Alert Number I-042425-PSA) describes cyber criminals using search engine advertisements that mimic legitimate business URLs with minimal differences, such as a minor misspelling, to direct victims to fake login pages.
  • The PSA notes criminals previously focused mainly on small business commercial bank account takeovers but have expanded to target payroll, unemployment programs, and health savings accounts to redirect payments via fraudulent wire transactions or direct deposit changes.

Where It Spreads

How to Protect Yourself

  • Navigate to employee self-service, payroll, unemployment, or retirement portals by typing the known URL directly or using a saved bookmark instead of clicking search engine ads
  • Carefully check the full web address for misspellings or subtle differences before entering login credentials
  • Enable multi-factor authentication on payroll, HSA, unemployment, and retirement accounts where available
  • Regularly review pay stubs and account statements for unauthorized direct deposit or banking changes
  • Report suspected incidents to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center at ic3.gov
