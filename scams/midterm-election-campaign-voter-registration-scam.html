Midterm Election Campaign & Voter Registration Scam

Added Oct 17, 2026· 🤖 AI-researched· Editor: Nick Elliott· Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) PSA, Help Net Security

Overview

Ahead of the 2026 U.S. midterms, the FBI warns scammers are impersonating candidates and campaigns using AI-cloned voices, fake political action committees, and phony voter-registration pages to steal donations, money, and personal data.

How It Works

The FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) issued a PSA warning that scammers are impersonating political campaigns, using AI-generated voices, images, and messages to create convincing fake solicitations for donations, undelivered merchandise, or voter registration schemes designed to harvest personally identifiable information

Red Flags

  • A text or email claims you are not registered to vote and links to an unfamiliar "voter registration" page asking for personal details
  • A "campaign" or PAC guarantees your donation will grow in value or be paid back as a financial return
  • Someone encourages you to recruit others to "invest" in a political fund to increase your payout share
  • Unsolicited calls or messages using a candidate's cloned voice, image, or slogan to pressure urgent donations
  • Pressure to act quickly, before you can verify the campaign or PAC through official channels

Real Examples

  • The FBI's October 15, 2026 PSA (an update to a 2024 election-cycle alert) described schemes where victims are promised financial returns on "investments" in campaign pools, only to lose funds or have their data compromised
  • A described voter-data scheme uses a text or email claiming the recipient isn't registered to vote and links to a fake state voter registration page to steal PII for identity theft
  • The FBI noted scammers use candidate names, images, logos and slogans to solicit fake campaign contributions and sell merchandise that is never delivered

Where It Spreads

Text MessagesEmailPhone CallsSocial Media

How to Protect Yourself

  • Verify any Political Action Committee, Party Committee, or Candidate Committee's registration and financial filings directly on the Federal Election Commission (FEC) website before donating
  • Remember that campaign donations are not investments \u2014 they will not increase in value or be returned
  • Never click links in unsolicited texts or emails about voter registration; go directly to your state's official election website to check or update your registration
  • Do not provide personally identifiable information to callers, texters, or emailers you don't know, even if they reference a candidate or election deadline
  • Be skeptical of merchandise offers tied to campaigns that require upfront payment with vague delivery promises
