Fake Trading App Scam

Added Dec 31, 2020· Source: FTC Consumer Alerts
Overview

Victims are convinced to download a fraudulent investment or trading app that shows fabricated, ever-increasing account balances, but withdrawals are blocked or additional "fees" are demanded before any money can be returned.

How It Works

A victim is persuaded — often by an online contact or advertisement — to download a trading or investment app that appears professional and fully functional. The app displays a fabricated account balance that climbs steadily as the victim deposits more money, creating the illusion of consistent profit, but when the victim tries to withdraw any funds, the app blocks the transaction or claims additional "taxes," "fees," or "verification deposits" must be paid first, a cycle that continues indefinitely while the deposited money is never actually invested anywhere.

Red Flags
  • An investment app was downloaded outside the official app store or recommended by an online contact.
  • The account balance grows rapidly and consistently with no losses.
  • Withdrawal requests are blocked or trigger a demand for an additional fee.
  • Customer support is unreachable except through the same contact who introduced the app.

How to Protect Yourself
  • Only download trading apps from official app stores, and verify the developer.
  • Try withdrawing a small amount early to test whether the platform is legitimate.
  • Be suspicious of any app that requires an additional fee before allowing a withdrawal.
  • Verify the platform is registered with financial regulators like the SEC or CFTC.
