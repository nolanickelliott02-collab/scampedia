5 new scams added to Scampedia this week. Tap one to see how it works and the red flags to watch for. Also see every documented scam.

Government Scam Nov 27, 2026

Fake IRS "Digital Asset Compliance Portal" Crypto Letter Scam

Scammers are mailing official-looking fake IRS letters to cryptocurrency holders demanding they "enroll" in a nonexistent Digital Asset Compliance Portal by scanning a QR code, which leads to a fraudulent site designed to steal personal data and crypto credentials.
Government Scam Nov 27, 2026

Fake "Tribal Tax Credit" Scheme

Promoters sell wealthy investors and tax professionals on a nonexistent "Tribal Tax Credit" (also marketed as a "Native American Tax Credit" or "Sovereign Tribal Tax Credit"), claiming it can wipe out federal tax bills or generate big refunds — but the IRS says no such credit exists under federal law.
Tech Scam Nov 28, 2026

Kali365 Device Code Phishing-as-a-Service Scam

A Telegram-distributed Phishing-as-a-Service kit called Kali365 tricks Microsoft 365 users into approving a "device code" that secretly hands attackers a working login session, letting criminals bypass MFA entirely without ever stealing a password.
Shopping Scam Nov 28, 2026

Third-Party "We'll Handle Your Return" Scam

Scammers advertise on social media offering to return or exchange unwanted holiday gifts for shoppers in exchange for a fee, then disappear with the merchandise and money instead of ever processing a refund.
Investment Scam Nov 26, 2026

NFT Airdrop Crypto Wallet Draining Scam

Criminals send unsolicited "free" NFT or token airdrops to cryptocurrency wallets, then lure recipients to a phishing site to "claim" or "connect" for more rewards — stealing the victim's seed phrase and draining the wallet.
