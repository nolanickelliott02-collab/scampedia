AI-Generated Fake Review Scam

Added Oct 24, 2026· 🤖 AI-researched· Editor: Nick Elliott· Source: Ohio Attorney General, Consumer Advocate Newsletter (December 2025)

Overview

Scammers and shady sellers use AI tools to mass-produce fake five-star product and service reviews that lure shoppers into buying low-quality goods or visiting fraudulent sites that steal payment and personal information.

How It Works

Fraudulent sellers and website operators use generative AI to write large batches of realistic-sounding customer reviews for products or services that were never actually purchased or used. These reviews are posted on shopping sites, marketplaces, and review platforms to build false credibility and push shoppers toward a purchase. In some cases the fake-review scheme is just the bait for a larger scam: the glowing reviews drive traffic to a fraudulent website that, once a buyer tries to check out or "verify" their order, harvests credit card numbers and other personal details, leading to identity theft and financial fraud rather than just a disappointing product.

Red Flags

  • Overly generic, gushing language with no specific details about how the product was used
  • Identical or nearly identical phrases repeated across multiple reviews for the same item
  • A sudden flood of five-star reviews posted within hours or days of each other
  • Reviewer profiles with only one or two reviews, often all five-star and for similar types of products
  • Reviews that sound 'too perfect' with no mention of downsides or imperfections

Real Examples

  • State consumer protection officials report that businesses are increasingly using AI to generate customer reviews that are not based on real purchases, sometimes as part of larger schemes where fraudulent websites use the fake reviews to lure shoppers into purchases or sign-ups that end in theft of personal or financial information.

Where It Spreads

Online MarketplacesShopping WebsitesSocial MediaReview Platforms

How to Protect Yourself

  • Click on a reviewer's name or profile to check their posting history; real users typically leave a mix of ratings across varied products
  • Be suspicious of listings with only glowing five-star reviews and no critical or mixed feedback
  • Watch for unnatural timing patterns, like dozens of five-star reviews appearing all at once
  • Research the seller or website independently before entering payment or personal information, especially if the purchase was driven mainly by reviews
  • Treat reviews as only one data point and verify a company through official channels before buying
